01Professional work

Multi-department business performance dashboard

ToolsPower BI

The problem

Business information was spread across different departments. Management needed a clearer way to understand sales, training, finance and customer support performance.

My work

I built Power BI dashboards covering five areas of the business:

  • Sales: sales figures, countries, programmes and customer interest.
  • Training: weekly class feedback, student understanding and practical application.
  • Finance: defaults, student drop-offs, retention, revenue and losses.
  • Customer support: complaints, ticket categories and resolution performance.
  • Management: key operational information brought together in one view.

Business value

Management can review important department figures without searching through separate reports. They can identify weak areas, monitor performance and direct attention towards issues affecting revenue, customer experience and daily operations.

02Professional work

Centralised Google Sheets and workflow management

ToolsGoogle SheetsApps Script

The problem

Information sat across multiple departmental spreadsheets. Teams had to search through different files to find updates, track work and understand what needed attention.

My work

I brought information from different departmental sheets into a central system.

I also created a request-tracking system for a content team, so staff can submit requests for graphics, carousels and editing work. It tracks requests, progress, workload and delivery timelines, with a dashboard view inside the Google Sheets workflow.

Business value

Teams have a clearer view of incoming work, pending requests and completed tasks. Managers can monitor workloads, follow progress and identify delays without repeatedly asking team members for updates.

03Professional work

Lead tracking and payment reconciliation

ToolsGoogle SheetsApps Script

The problem

When leads and payments are tracked across different records, it is harder to know which prospects need follow-up, who has paid and where payment records need attention.

My work

I developed a system for tracking leads and organising payment information. It brings lead activity and payment records into a more structured view.

Business value

The team gets better visibility into lead progress and payment status. This supports follow-ups, reduces time spent checking separate records and helps the business identify outstanding payments.

04Training delivered

Fleet analytics and data training

ToolsPower BI

The problem

Working with large fleet datasets requires people to understand how to organise, analyse and interpret operational information.

My work

I delivered a five-day Power BI training programme for graduate trainees working with fleet data covering 1,000 trucks. The programme introduced them to working with fleet information and presenting findings through Power BI.

Business value

The training helped graduate trainees develop practical skills for analysing fleet information and communicating findings. This supports better reporting and gives the team more people who can work with operational data.

05Professional work

Student feedback analysis and automated reporting

Toolsn8n

The problem

With thousands of student comments and weekly class feedback coming in, reading every response manually takes time, and recurring complaints or positive feedback are hard to track.

My work

I built an n8n workflow that processes student feedback, identifies recurring themes and organises positive and negative comments. It sends weekly email reports to training department heads.

Business value

Department heads receive organised feedback without manually reviewing every comment. They can spot recurring concerns, follow up on issues and understand how students are responding to training.

06Professional work

SQL reporting and database management

ToolsSQLPower BIGoogle Sheets

The problem

Business information stored in spreadsheets becomes difficult to manage as reporting needs grow. Teams need a more structured way to organise data and retrieve information.

My work

I moved data from Google Sheets into a database, created SQL views for Power BI reporting and wrote queries to retrieve sales and payment-default information.

Business value

Reporting now runs on a more structured data source. Teams can retrieve the information they need through SQL and use it in Power BI reports, which makes sales and defaults easier to review.
